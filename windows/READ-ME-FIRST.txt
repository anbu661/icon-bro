BRO — your desktop buddy (Windows)
=================================

INSTALL
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (for example Documents\Bro).
2. Double-click Bro.exe.

FIRST OPEN (one time only)
Bro isn't from the Microsoft Store, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC":
click "More info", then "Run anyway". After that, Bro opens normally.

WHAT BRO DOES
• Lives on your desktop, walks around, waves hello. Biscuit the cat is the default buddy.
• Reminds you to drink water (every 45 min). Click YES and he does a happy run.
• When you open YouTube, Instagram, Netflix, Prime Video, Hotstar and more in your browser, he asks
  how much time you need, hides, then nags you and closes the tab when your time is up.
• Tidies folders: right-click Bro > Tidy a folder. Images, videos, audio, documents and archives go
  into their own folders. Nothing is deleted, and "Undo last tidy" puts everything back.
  Works offline: no AI, no internet needed.

CLICKS
• Click Bro: he shows off a swag move.   • Double-click: opens chat.
• Drag him anywhere.   • Right-click: everything else (character, tidy, hide, settings, quit).
• When he hides, click the little "Bro" pill at the bottom-right of the screen to bring him back.

CHARACTERS
Right-click Bro > Character to switch between Biscuit the Cat, Bro (Denim), Iron Man, Drop and Buddy.
You can also change it in Settings.

FIRST RUN
The first time Bro opens, he gives you a quick tour (Next > Next > Got it).
Replay it any time: right-click Bro > Show tutorial.

CHAT
Double-click Bro to chat. Without any setup he understands simple commands offline:
"tidy my downloads", "tidy my desktop", "undo", "I drank water", "walk left".

AGENTIC CHAT (optional)
For real conversations, right-click Bro > Settings > Agentic chat, choose your provider
(OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, or any OpenAI-compatible service) and paste your own
API key. The key is stored only on your PC. Bro ships with no keys.

SETTINGS
Right-click Bro > Settings... to change:
• what Bro calls you, the character, and the water reminder interval
• which sites and OTT apps to watch
• the time choices, and the default time if you don't answer
• walking around, start with Windows, and agentic chat

Works on Windows 10 and 11.
