BRO — your desktop buddy
========================

INSTALL
1. Drag Bro into the Applications folder (in this window).
2. Open Applications and double-click Bro.

FIRST OPEN (one time only)
Bro isn't from the App Store, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time:
1. Double-click Bro. macOS says it "could not verify" Bro. Click Done.
2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
3. Scroll down to "Bro was blocked…" and click "Open Anyway".
4. Enter your Mac password, then click "Open".
After that, Bro opens normally.

(Alternative for Terminal users:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Bro.app )

WHAT BRO DOES
• Lives on your desktop, walks around, waves hello.
• Reminds you to drink water (every 45 min). Click YES and he does a happy run.
• When you open YouTube, Instagram, Netflix, Prime Video, Hotstar and more, he asks how much
  time you need, then nags you and closes the tab when your time is up.
  (macOS asks "Bro wants to control Google Chrome / Safari" — click OK, or he can't see your tabs.)
• Tidies folders: right-click Bro → Tidy a folder. Images, videos, audio, documents and
  archives go into their own folders. Nothing is deleted, and "Undo last tidy" puts everything back.
  Works offline — no AI, no internet needed.

CLICKS
• Click Bro: he shows off a swag move.   • Double-click: opens chat.
• Drag him anywhere.   • Right-click: everything else (tidy, minimise, settings, quit).
• Minimise: right-click → Minimise Bro. Bring him back from the 😎 in the menu bar or the Dock.

CHARACTERS
Biscuit the cat is the default buddy. Right-click Bro → Character to switch to Bro (Denim),
Iron Man, Drop or Buddy. You can also change it in Settings.

FIRST RUN
The first time Bro opens, he gives you a quick tour (Next → Next → Got it).
Replay it any time: right-click Bro → Show tutorial.

CHAT
Double-click Bro to chat. Without any setup he understands simple commands offline:
"tidy my downloads", "tidy my desktop", "undo", "I drank water", "walk left".

AGENTIC CHAT (optional)
For real conversations, right-click Bro → Settings → Agentic chat, choose your provider
(OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, or any OpenAI-compatible service) and paste your own
API key. The key is stored only on your Mac. Bro ships with no keys.

SETTINGS
Right-click Bro → Settings… to change:
• what Bro calls you, the character, and the water reminder interval
• which sites and OTT apps to watch (YouTube, Instagram, Netflix, Prime Video, JioHotstar, …)
• the time choices, and the default time if you don't answer
• walking around, start at login, and agentic chat
Changes apply straight away.

Works on macOS 13 Ventura or newer, Apple Silicon and Intel.
